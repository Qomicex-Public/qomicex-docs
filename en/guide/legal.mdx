## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

Qomicex Launcher (hereinafter "this software") is developed and maintained by the Qomicex team. We take your privacy seriously, and this policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

### 1. Legal notice

- This software has no affiliation, partnership, or endorsement relationship with **Mojang Studios / Microsoft** or their products.
- **Minecraft** and related names, logos, and copyrights are owned by **Mojang Studios / Microsoft**.
- This software does not include the Minecraft game itself; the copyright of game assets belongs to their respective owners.
- By using this software, you confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Scope of data collection

This software may collect the following information:

| Data type | Description | Purpose | Anonymous |
|---------|------|---------|---------|
| Microsoft account credentials | Used for Minecraft authentication (Microsoft login method only) | Authentication | No (stored locally) |
| Plugin error telemetry | Plugin ID, version, error type | Improve the plugin ecosystem | Yes |
| Crash reports | Error stack, device information (OS, CPU, memory) | Diagnosis and fixes | No (contains device information) |
| Crash logs | Minecraft crash logs, game logs | Problem diagnosis | No (contains path information) |
| Launcher version | Current version number | Update checks | Yes |
| License information | Hardware fingerprint + license token | License verification | No (license-required builds only) |

### 3. How data is used

- **Account credentials**: used only to authenticate with Microsoft/Minecraft services and never transmitted to third parties
- **Error telemetry**: used to improve the stability of the plugin ecosystem; fully anonymous and free of personally identifiable information
- **Crash reports**: used to diagnose and fix software problems, sent with your consent or when a critical error occurs
- **Crash log sharing**: when you run crash analysis, the system automatically uploads the crash log to the third-party mclo.gs service to generate a shareable link
- **Version information**: used only to check for software updates
- **License information**: used only to verify your authorization to use the software (license-required builds only)

### 4. Data storage and retention

- **Account data**: stored on your local device; you may choose to delete it when uninstalling the software
- **Crash reports**: retained for 90 days, then deleted automatically
- **Telemetry data**: retained for 365 days, then anonymized automatically
- **License information**: retained for as long as the license is valid

### 5. Data security

- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
- HTTPS encryption is used in transit
- Sensitive data stored locally (such as tokens) is encrypted at rest
- We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties

### 6. Third-party services

This software interacts with the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Data transmitted | Privacy policy |
|-----|------|---------|---------|
| Microsoft | Account authentication | OAuth token | [https://privacy.microsoft.com](https://privacy.microsoft.com) |
| mclo.gs | Crash log sharing | Crash log content | [https://mclo.gs/privacy](https://mclo.gs/privacy) |
| api.qomicex.top | Update checks, error reports | Version information, crash reports | This policy applies |
| plugins.qomicex.top | Plugin store | Plugin queries, install records | This policy applies |

### 7. Cookies and local storage

- This software uses local storage (LocalStorage) to save your preferences
- This software does not use browser cookies
- Locally stored data is not accessed by third parties

### 8. Cross-border data transfer

- This software primarily operates within China
- The crash log sharing service (mclo.gs) may involve cross-border data transfer
- Using third-party services indicates your consent to the relevant data transfer

### 9. Your rights

- **Right of access**: you may view locally stored account data at any time
- **Right of deletion**: you may delete locally stored account data at any time
- **Right of control**: you may turn automatic error reporting and plugin error telemetry on or off in the settings at any time
- **Withdrawal of consent**: you may withdraw your consent to this Privacy Policy at any time

### 10. Protection of minors

This software does not collect personal information from minors under the age of 14. If you are a minor, please use this software under the guidance of a guardian.

### 11. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published within this software. For significant changes, we will notify you inside the software.
